    About

    Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry is one of the most important schools of magic in the world and is located in the Scottish Highlands. For over 1000 years, magically Any magical children residing in Great Britain or Ireland who has reached the age of eleven by the start of the incoming term are accepted as students.

    School's coat of arms represents the four houses named after the school's founders, in which the students live during their time at Hogwarts. Below this, a parchment scroll shows the school motto.
    Draco dormiens nunquam titillandus (Never tickle a sleeping dragon).
